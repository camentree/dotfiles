Assistant

    How this page works

    Every piece of work is shown by whose hands it is in. Facts come from GitHub every 2 minutes while the page is open, agents write what they know about their task, and the page saves what you do here: ticks, order, the Day, answers.

    Status: five sections in the order to work them, then Start

    1 Questions
    An agent, or the assistant itself, asked you a question, or an agent stopped. It does nothing more until you answer. The Status tab carries a red dot while a question here has no answer. Each question has an "agent" button that opens that agent's session in Ghostty.
    2 Merge
    Every pull request of yours that GitHub calls approved and where nothing is left for you but the merge, whatever its agent is doing. Ready: mergeable now, the only place merge is enabled. Blocked: waiting on something that is not yours to fix now, with the reason as the card's last line (checks running, waits for #84, do not merge label). A frontend pull request waits for its backend half; the backend half does not wait for it. Then Complete: merged today. In merge order. One approval short of GitHub's decision is not here; it shows "1 approval" where it is. The page never merges by itself.
    3 Review
    A teammate's pull request that asks for you by name or for the move-ins team (drafts wait until they are ready for review), one you reviewed, or one an agent is preparing for you. Other teams' requests are left out.
    4 PRs
    Every open pull request of yours, drafts included. The ones you are reviewing sit on top with no heading, then Queued (prepared for you), Waiting for Me (drafts, open threads, requested changes), Waiting for Review, Blocked and Complete. Failing checks alone never put one in front of you: they show only as a reason on an approved pull request in Merge → Blocked.
    5 Ask
    Your pull requests to ask someone to review now: in each stack the earliest one no teammate has reviewed, plus more in a deep stack so no more than three unreviewed ones wait un-asked. Tick it once you asked.
    6 Start
    Tasks nobody has started (no agent, no pull request), grouped by project in the Projects tab's order, each in its project's own order, with its size, the project's due date in the heading, and its start button. The first five of a longer project show, with "and N more" for the rest. Its number is how many there are; nothing here needs you, so it never adds a red dot. Snoozed and dismissed tasks stay out. Dragging here reorders the project, as on the Projects tab.

    The number on a section counts the cards that still need you. Review and Address are split into groups with their own counts; click a group's name to fold it, and the page remembers. Review: In Progress (an agent or your own unsent review is on it, or the author answered your review: pushed, replied in a thread, or asked you again), High Priority (urgent, or asked only of you and waiting over 2 days), Backlog (everything else waiting for your review), then Complete (you reviewed and it is in the author's hands; a card you ticked there comes back unticked when the author answers). Address: In Progress (an agent is on it or its fixes wait for you), Waiting for Me, and Waiting for Review (waiting on a reviewer, nothing for you). Every section ends with a folded Complete group, not counted, holding what is done: answered, merged today, fixes pushed with nothing left for you today, reviewers asked, reviews you submitted. Questions, Merge and Address clear it at midnight; a card comes back out as soon as it needs you again.

    Cards keep their place: a refresh never reorders a list, a new card goes to the end, and only your drag moves one. The exception is your own pull requests in Address and Merge: they are in merge order (a pull request after the one it sits on or waits for, stacks in stack order, "3/10" on each), which changes only when a dependency does; a drag there holds until it does. When a card leaves a list, a grey copy of it, as it was, stays in its place until you remove it. The pill on its top edge says where it went (Complete, Waiting for Me, merged…): click it to go to the card. The round × removes the copy; ticking its box (the only live thing in it) ticks the real card and removes the copy too. A card that moves again updates its copy, and one that comes back removes it. Past three copies in a group the rest sit behind "and N more moved", with "× all". None for a card you ticked done. Every move is also listed in the sync arrows' history.

    A card

    Title
    Opens the pull request or ticket. The tags say backend or frontend.
    Robot line
    Under the buttons: the agent on this card in a few words (working, reviewing, ready; red when it waits for you or stopped; faint "finished 2h ago" when it is done and nothing runs). Hover for its whole line; the robot at the top right opens the session. With no agent it shows where the card stands. After you press a button the same line shows what happened to the press, then goes back to the agent's status.
    Facts line
    Number and the branch it merges into, author, age, size, CI, reviews, then the standing: one phrase for where it stands and for how long.
    Arrow
    Points right when the card is closed, down when open. Opens the proof (recordings, screenshots) and the tabs: Explanation (what the agent is doing, the explanation, and pictures taken before any work of where the feature goes or what the bug is; click one to enlarge), the question, the decisions the agent made without asking, and its own review. Every card looks the same closed, wherever it is.
    Tick
    Yours only: the card stays where it is but shrinks to its title row, the title in grey (a red dot if something on it still needs you; click the title or the arrow to open the whole card). The section's number stops counting it, and the assistant checks it is really done. If something puts it back in your hands later (the author answered your review, a new review or comment on yours, checks start failing, it became mergeable, an agent asks you something or stops), the tick clears by itself and the card says "unticked: …" and why for a few minutes. On an Ask card it records that you asked the reviewer instead.
    Drag
    Drag cards to reorder a list.

    Buttons

    A card always shows every button its kind can have, in the same order. A faint dashed one is not available yet: hover it for why. Hover any button for what it does.

    Any pull request number on the page (#87) is a link. Hover it for its title, whose it is, where it sits on the page, its standing and checks. Click it to go to its card, unfolding the group; when it has no card here it opens on GitHub, and cmd- or ctrl-click always opens GitHub.

    After a press, a line on the card says what is happening: first that it is going through, then what the server did with it, then a tick once the page has seen it actually happen (the agent is working, the review is open, the pull request merged). If it was delivered but the agent has not reported anything yet, the line says so quietly in grey; if the server gave no answer at all (or none within 30 seconds), it says "no answer from the server, checking…", never presses again for you, and keeps checking whether it happened anyway, and only says so in red when nothing showed up; red means it was refused, failed to deliver, or the agent stopped, and stays until you clear it or what it complained about comes true after all, when it turns into the tick by itself.

    answer
    On a card whose agent asked you something: opens the question with its options and a box for your words. Open by default in Questions.
    The row
    Every card has queue, review, block, to queue, dismiss (a Merge card has merge first). A button that cannot act yet is dashed, and its tooltip says why. The robot at the card's top right opens its Claude session in Ghostty.
    queue
    Starts an agent that prepares what you need to review (addresses comments, fixes CI, or puts the review together); it shows "preparing" and lands in Queued when ready. Off once something is prepared.
    to queue
    Moves a card you are reviewing, or a blocked one, back to Queued. No agent is started.
    block
    Type in a line what it is blocked on: it moves to Blocked, the reason goes in its comments and to the assistant, which unblocks it when the reason no longer holds.
    Tick
    Done: the card stays ticked while the assistant checks. Done for real, it moves to Complete; not done, it comes back unticked with a red border and the reason.
    Pencil and speech bubble
    Small icons at the card's top right, beside the arrow. The pencil (with the count once the card has comments) is comment: a private reminder to yourself, never sent to any agent or posted anywhere. Both icons show when you hover the card; the pencil always shows, with a count, once the card has comments. Clicking it opens a dropdown: a box to add one (Enter saves, Shift+Enter starts a new line), then your comments newest first with when you wrote them, edit and remove. An outside click, Escape or a second click closes it. It stays with the card wherever it goes until you remove it, and the pencil's tooltip shows the newest, also on a ticked card. It never moves a card or changes a tick. The speech bubble is message: it opens the chat with this card attached as a chip above the field (press it on more cards to attach several). Type what you want ("complete this") and send; the message carries the card, and the chip in the sent bubble jumps to it. Both stay on a ticked card.
    review
    Opens every page prepared for the review in one Safari tab group and moves it to In Progress (the Now tab). On in every Queued card: one with nothing recorded to open asks its agent to open what it prepared instead. Send comments is on the bar above difit.
    merge
    Merges and deletes the branch. Asks once more first.
    dismiss
    Hides the card for good. Hidden cards are listed under the section to restore.
    start
    Starts an agent on a task you added by hand.

    Top of the page

    Sync arrows
    GitHub sync: green when it last synced within 5 minutes, red when later or when the last sync failed (then the page shows what it read last), turning while a sync runs. Click for the history (when, what changed, how long) and "sync now".
    Robot
    How many agent sessions are busy right now, with a red dot when one is waiting for you (ready, a question, an approval). Lists the live sessions under the section their card is in, and the finished ones in a folded "Finished" group at the bottom, newest first; pick one to open it in a terminal (the row says "opened ✓" or why not), or → to go to its card.
    Speech bubble
    Agents' questions, with a red dot while one is open. Each open question is its own block under "Needs your answer": answer right there (the list stays open for the next one), or click the question to go to its card. After you send, your answer stays in its block with what happened to it, until the agent has actually picked it up; only then it moves down to Answered, the quieter list below, where a click shows the whole question and your answer.
    Day, Water, Stand
    Day is how much of 8 am to 6 pm has gone. Each click on Water or Stand adds one and moves its bar one step toward 8 for the day; Option-click, press and hold, or the "−" beside it takes the last one off. Under the bar, when you last marked it; it turns red when that was over an hour ago.

    The other tabs

    Now
    Only what you are reviewing right now: every card you pressed review on and have not queued, blocked or finished.
    Projects
    Each project's progress and due date. Open one for its cards, and add a task by hand at the bottom. Each project has its own row of tabs, with counts (faint when empty): All, Not Started (a task with no agent and no pull request), then the Status sections. All shows every card with a pill saying where it stands; click the pill to go to the card on Status. The page remembers the tab you chose per project. Drag to reorder in any tab: in a tab that shows only some cards, the dragged card lands right after the card now above it (or right before the one below, at the top) in the project's one order.
    PRs
    Every open pull request in one list: yours, and the ones waiting on others, with stack position and age (red after 4 days).
    + at the bottom right
    The Log: a chat with the assistant, a background session that posts what changed when it starts and every hour, and acts on what you write: says where something stands, adds a task, passes an instruction to an agent, starts work. It never merges, pushes or posts under your name. The robot icon in its header opens the assistant's own session in Ghostty (on the machine running this page), with a ✓ beside it once opened or a red "retry" when it was not. It opens from any tab, with a red dot when the assistant wrote since you last looked. Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line. Under your message it says sending, then delivered, then ✓ once it is in the Log, and dots show until the assistant writes back, for up to two minutes.

    Reviewing

    A card's difit link opens difit under a thin bar with one button, "agent", which opens the agent's chat in Ghostty (on the machine running this page). The tab is named after the card. While you review, the agent watches difit and answers each comment you post as it lands: first a line saying what it will do, then the result.

    To publish a teammate's review, write it in difit: start a comment with post: for each one that should go to GitHub, and leave one verdict comment starting accept:, changes: or comment: followed by the summary: the agent posts the review once it reads the verdict. On your own pull requests the same goes for fixes and replies: say "looks good" (or post:, accept:, changes:, comment:) in difit, and the agent pushes and posts them. If it finds one of your comments factually off, it asks you first: the question shows on the card and in the speech bubble, with options such as posting it revised (the revision in full), posting it as written, or dropping it, and a box for your own wording.
