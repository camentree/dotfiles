Editable Recommended Care Rate

backend PR #8100 frontend PR #9661 BILL-2798 proof page
    What the PR does

    Before. A prospect, Ruth, is assessed before moving in at Level 2 care, which costs $1,200 a month. That $1,200 is her recommended care rate. Her move-in agreement can only print $1,200, and when she signs, billing tells staff "add a $1,200 care charge for Ruth." But Ruth is on Medicaid (Elderly Waiver): the community charges her nothing for care, and staff had no way to say so.

    After. A new field on the agreement, Editable Recommended Care Rate, starts out at $1,200. Staff change it:

    • to $0 for Ruth: the agreement prints $0.00, the "rent + care" total leaves care out, and no care charge is recommended;
    • to $900 for a negotiated price: the agreement and total use $900, and billing recommends a $900 care charge;
    • or leave it alone: everything behaves as today.

    A reassessment after move-in goes back to the care level's own rate.

    The thread through everything you raised: the PR folds two ideas into one number. The assessment gives the price of care. The agreement says what the resident is billed. One field holding both shows up as:

    • a name ("editable") that doesn't say which of the two it is, so a reader can't tell when to use it;
    • an agreement and a reassessment that keep recommending different amounts, because the move-in amount stays saved;
    • $0 not ending a care charge the resident already has;
    • on the frontend: a typed amount equal to the recommended one isn't saved, and the new field shares its label with the existing one.
